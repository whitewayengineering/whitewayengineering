### About

I build reliable firmware for STM32-based devices: drivers, RTOS applications and communication stacks that keep working in the field. I am a senior embedded engineer with 5+ years of experience taking products from first prototype to series production, across industrial control, energy and battery systems, vehicle communication and connected devices.

Whiteway Engineering is my freelance practice, based in Türkiye and working with clients worldwide. I take on firmware and hardware projects of any size, including unusual ones.

### What I work with

| Area | Tools and technologies |

| RTOS | FreeRTOS, Azure RTOS (ThreadX) |

### Current project

**Whiteway Mainboard (rev A)** · a modular STM32H7 development board  
STM32H742/H743 in LQFP100, 4-layer PCB up to 100 × 100 mm, two mikroBUS sockets and ten custom module connectors for Wi-Fi/BLE, LoRa, Ethernet, RS-485, CAN, display, SD card and I/O. Schematic, firmware and a 3D-printed enclosure are in progress; files will be published here once the first revision is ready.

### Services

- STM32 firmware debugging: HardFaults, DMA and interrupt issues, RTOS timing problems
- Modbus RTU/TCP, CAN and J1939 integration
- Peripheral drivers and FreeRTOS application structure
- Custom bootloaders and firmware updates over UART, CAN or USB
- Firmware code review with a written report
